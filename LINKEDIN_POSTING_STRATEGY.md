## Posting Schedule Recommendation

### Option A: Aggressive (3 posts per week)
- Complete in 5 weeks
- Maintain momentum
- High visibility

### Option B: Moderate (2 posts per week)
- Complete in 7-8 weeks
- Allow for engagement
- More sustainable

### Option C: Conservative (1 post per week)
- Complete in 15 weeks
- Build anticipation
- Deepen each conversation

---

## Best Practices for Each Post

### 1. **Hook** (First 2 lines)
- Ask question, make bold statement, or share surprising fact
- Example: "I've built 50+ projects across 6 programming languages..."

### 2. **Context** (Next 3-5 lines)
- Why this project matters
- Problem it solved
- What you learned

### 3. **Content** (Main body)
- Use 3-5 key points with emojis
- Include numbers/metrics when possible
- Be specific, not generic

### 4. **Call-to-Action** (Last 1-2 lines)
- Ask for comments/suggestions
- Request connections from similar interests
- Link to GitHub/deployed project

### 5. **Formatting**
- Use line breaks liberally
- Bullet points > dense paragraphs
- Emojis for visual interest (not excessive)
- Hashtags: 5-10 relevant ones

## Engagement Strategy

### Days to Post
- **Best:** Tuesday-Thursday (9-10 AM, 5-6 PM in your timezone)
- **Engage:** First 2 hours are critical for algorithm

### Initial Engagement
- Respond to every comment in first 4 hours
- Like/comment on 5-10 relevant posts daily
- Share others' content (not just your own)

### Follow-up Posts
- Repost successful content after 1-2 months
- Create "compilation" posts of related projects
- Share lessons from failed projects
